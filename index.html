Keep it going

Bat care basics

A few habits that help a refurbished bat last.

Don't over-oil

A light coat is plenty. Too much oil softens the willow and can hurt performance.

Store it right

Keep your bat at room temperature, away from extreme heat, damp and the boot of a hot car.

Protect the toe

A toe guard helps keep moisture out of the bottom of the blade, where bats wear fastest.

Check it often

Look over the handle, grip and edges regularly. Small cracks are easier to fix early.

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Use the contact section below.

Do I pay online?

No. There is no online payment. You choose your package and book it in, then pay in person when you hand your bat over to us.

Where do you operate?

Adelaide metro, South Australia only. We do local pick-up and drop-off. We don't offer postal or shipping.

What happens after I submit my booking?

Your booking opens in WhatsApp or email, ready to send. Attach your bat photos in the chat. One of our team will then message you within 24 hours to confirm your price and sort out pick-up/drop-off.

What if my bat has serious damage?

Severe cleft cracks or broken handles are quoted separately after we check your photos.

I'm under 18. Can I book?

Yes. Please make sure a parent/guardian is aware of the booking. You'll be asked to confirm this before you submit.

Can I book more than one bat?

Yes. Set the number of bats in the form. Booking 3 or more together applies the Club/Mate Bundle: 10% off your total.

How do promo codes work?

Enter a code in the booking form and hit Apply, or tap a code in the offers section. One code per booking, and discounts don't stack: you automatically get whichever saves you the most.

What does the knocking-in advice cover?

With the Repair & Protect and Full Rebuild packages, we'll give you advice on caring for and knocking in your bat when you get it back.
